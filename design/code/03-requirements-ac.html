subject Background & Context
JIRA spec baseline

Stripe Tax calculations can fail or time out under peak load or network partition; this service introduces an automated fallback to cached local tax tables with an async tax reconciliation queue to prevent double-charging or retry storms. Under baseline operation, calls failing past the SLA budget will degrade gracefully into buffered transactions tagged for downstream audit reconciliation.
